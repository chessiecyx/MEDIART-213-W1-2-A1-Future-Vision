What Makes Us Human?

A reflection on humanity, kindness, and AI.

01. The Human Experience

Humans can connect with the world in ways that AI cannot. We can feel the warmth of sunlight, the softness of an animal's fur, and emotions that come from our experiences. AI may understand these things through information, but it cannot truly experience them the way we do. I will begin by exploring the world through my own senses, building real connections, and letting these experiences shape my dreams.

02. Memories of Kindness

I hope people will remember me for the meaningful things I have done, whether it is helping stray animals or encouraging someone who needs support. I want to leave behind memories of kindness and the positive impact I have made on others.

03. What Remains

Even if AI rewrites my story, the things I have truly done will not disappear. The kindness I have shared will still have a real impact on the people and animals I have helped.

04. AI as My Tool

I hope AI can help me do more meaningful things in the future, but I do not want to depend on it. AI should be a tool that supports my ideas, not something that controls the way I think. My choices, values, and dreams should always remain my own. I will question its suggestions, check its results, and always make the final decisions myself.
