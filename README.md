# What Makes Us Human?
Yixuan Chen | Fall 2026

## Concept

This project explores the differences between human experiences and artificial intelligence. Through the themes of touch, memory, kindness, and personal choice, I reflect on what makes us human and what AI cannot truly experience.

The work emphasizes that meaningful actions and genuine connections leave lasting impacts, even when the stories about us change. AI can be a useful tool for achieving our goals, but our thoughts, values, and decisions should remain our own.

## Visual Direction

I chose warm colors, handwritten-style typography, and imagery of human connection to create a personal and comforting atmosphere.
